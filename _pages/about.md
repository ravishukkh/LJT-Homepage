I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, where I work with Professor Junxian He as a member of the **HKUST NLP Group**. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

Before starting my Ph.D., I graduated from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He.

Research Interests
======
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
